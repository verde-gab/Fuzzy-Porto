# Fuzzy-Porto Research Notes — Index

> Standalone Obsidian vault for notes on papers and books used in the
> Fuzzy Time Series × Santos Port research.

## How to Use

Each `.md` file is named after its BibTeX key (or a descriptive slug for books
without one). The YAML frontmatter links back to the original PDF and the
extracted text file.

### Template for a new note

```markdown
---
bibtex_key: Author_Year
title: "Full title of the paper/book"
authors: Author Name(s)
year: YYYY
tags: [topic1, topic2]
pdf: "[[pdf_research/path/to/file.pdf]]"
txt: "[[txt/path/to/file.txt]]"
---

# Author (Year) — Short Title

## Key Ideas
-

## Methodology
-

## Relevance to My Research
-

## Quotes / Highlights
-

## Open Questions
-
```
